# Demo flow

## Detect → Investigate → Explain → Act → Learn

1. Start the app and open the command center. Default local mode creates 10,000 deterministic synthetic borrowers.
2. Select **Risk alerts**, search `BRW-DEMO-001`, and open Sundar Harvest Collective.
3. Review the HIGH tier and the receivable, cash-flow, repayment, and concentration components. Revenue remains broadly stable.
4. Choose **Run investigation**. The deterministic agent retrieves profile, exposure, repayment, cash-flow, ageing, buyer, business, historical, event, and score evidence.
5. Inspect the counter-evidence and recommended human follow-up.
6. Choose **Scenario simulation** to show the illustrative score after the two largest receivables are collected. Treat it as a what-if, not a forecast.
7. Go to **Interventions**, enter `BRW-DEMO-001`, record a review action, then open the borrower again to see intervention history.

Other seeded borrowers: `BRW-DEMO-002` healthy seasonal borrower, `BRW-DEMO-003` buyer concentration, `BRW-DEMO-004` structural deterioration, `BRW-DEMO-005` transaction anomaly, `BRW-DEMO-006` temporary seasonal stress and recovery.
